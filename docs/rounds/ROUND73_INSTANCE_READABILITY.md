# Round 73: equipment copies, combat and shrine readability

Base `6f4aad8`, branch `sol2/work`; changes confined to `botw-clone-sol2`.

Gear shortcuts now store stable copy identities. A bound dry second branch, Pot Lid or Traveler's Bow equips that copy and shows its shortcut badge only there. Identities persist through reorder and save/reload. Dropping, throwing, burning or otherwise removing the bound copy **clears its shortcut**, even with another copy available. Pickup creates a new identity; any automatic weapon/bow shortcut names the new pickup. Food shortcuts still refer to a stack and clear after its final portion is consumed. Legacy saves bind the first matching owned copy once; incomplete, duplicate or dangling identity records are rejected.

Small flame and droplet marks distinguish burning and wet inventory copies without new text. Waiting/approaching Bokoblins carry their weapons low; windup/attack retain the raised contact pose. The sword trail is narrower, softer and shorter (75 ms). Oman Au holds its complete arrival tableau longer (4.4-second reveal); the camera check verifies over two fully revealed seconds with weight, plate and bridge visible at 30/60/120 Hz. Keh Namut has brighter moving streaks toward the receiving plate and a blue receiver matching the crate. The optional frost cistern retains its original inlet ripples. Stage progress remains chime/glow only.

**Validation: 21 targeted groups passed**, including combat/contact geometry and production waiting poses (idle/walk/run, tips above ground), input, integration (1,316 assertions), save and fresh-process/status/weather/Talus persistence, slots, elements, cooking, trees, fauna, combat music, shrine camera/shaft/lighting/ceremony, and all four normal-input shrine routes. Existing slot, save, combat and arrival checks were extended; no runner entry was needed. Three integration expectations were updated for the clear-on-disappearance rule. The entire 102-group suite was not rerun.
