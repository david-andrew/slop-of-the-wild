# Round 69 sol2: suppress flat grass-fire sheets

Follow-up to gate-rejected `a109a7f`. Work stays on `sol2/work`.

## Changes

Contour cards are absent inside 9 m, where the existing camera-facing tongues
and glow supply the approved close effect; their opacity blends in over 9–12 m.
Beyond that near exclusion, contour sheets turn toward the camera, with a minimum standing
rise and a small camera-up contribution for downward views. This keeps the
connected front above its glow instead of laying a low world-aligned panel
across sloping dirt. At 12–26 m the sheets smoothly return to their fuel-boundary
tangents. Tangent signs are aligned before this blend, so an opposite camera
heading cannot cancel the sheet width midway through the transition.

Contour visibility uses the full 3D sight angle, including camera elevation,
and reaches zero before grazing sheets collapse. The CPU skips fully hidden
segments; the vertex shader fades all four corners together. A smaller contour
core reduces the bright stripe through the standing flame body.

The connecting billboard mass now also obeys the actual, camera-bounded sheet
aspect. Previously its distance LOD bypassed the short-sheet guard, leaving a
broad orange parallelogram behind surviving narrow tongues. Compressed sheets
now retain only those upright tongues. The existing upper tongue geometry, body light, loincloth falloff, cooking
logs and irregular contour crest function are unchanged by this follow-up.

The registered `task69_fire_check` exercises all four submitted contour corners
in face-on, side-on and overhead views, a continuous angular sweep, and the
actual fragment shader at three compressed aspects and four seeds with the
full distance LOD enabled.
