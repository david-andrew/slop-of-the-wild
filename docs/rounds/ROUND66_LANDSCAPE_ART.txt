Round 66 landscape art
Baseline: master 48285bf. Branch: sol3/work. No push or merge.

Ranked critique and implemented changes:
1. Bare glide cliff
   Heightfield ribs and recessed gullies break the off-route shoulder; cool mineral washes and broader relief shading carry its exposed rock.
2. Stripy west cliff strata
   Closer stone values, broad cross-bed stains and broken lip highlights let buttress lighting lead. The waterfall remains the focal light shape.
3. Evenly bright Temple slopes
   Baked damp hollows separate cool swathes from warm dry lawns. Existing shrubs form longer uneven groups and remain visible from the overview.
4. Blobby snow boundary
   Wind tongues, baked ridge exposure and stronger mixed-margin scour reveal rock/tundra between drifts. Deep snow stays continuous.
5. Repeated procedural slope texture
   Distant comb and small-stroke contrast is reduced. Broader meadow masses read first; some evenly scattered original trees remain.
