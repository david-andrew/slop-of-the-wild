# Three original title themes

Written for the existing piano and the actual camera: cue time 0 is the crest;
crane/gaze opens at 5–9 seconds, lettering fades in at 9–9.85, camera continues
rising to 13, title fades at 16–18.4, control returns at 18.9. No camera changes.
All pitches below are sounding pitch (middle C = C4). Durations fill each bar;
`r` means rest. These are original melodies, not transcriptions of Zelda music.

## A — Farther than home (recommended)

D minor, **3/4, quarter = 80**, eight bars beginning at 0.25 s. Singing short–long–
short–short fourth-leap hook, answered by a descending line. Four-bar question,
four-bar answer; the leap grows from a fourth to a fifth and the answer moves into
the upper register. Climax at **9.25 s**, release through about 20 s. Solo piano.

| Bar / start | Melody (quarter-note beats) | Harmony / left hand | Dynamic |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 / 0.25 | A4 .5, D5 1.5, E5 .5, C5 .5 | Dm(add9): D3–A3–F4, spread | p, lean into D |
| 2 / 2.50 | A4 1, G4 .5, F4 .5, E4 1 | Bbmaj7: Bb2–F3–D4 | p, descending taper |
| 3 / 4.75 | A4 .5, D5 1.5, F5 .5, E5 .5 | Gm6: G2–D3–Bb3 | mp, rise |
| 4 / 7.00 | Eb5 1, Bb4 1, r 1 | Eb major (bII): Eb3–Bb3–G4; damp early | diminish, breath |
| 5 / 9.25 | D5 .5, A5 1.5, G5 .5, E5 .5 | D2/D3–A3–E4, no third; melody octaves | mf, broadest statement |
| 6 / 11.50 | F#5 1, E5 .5, D5 .5, A4 1 | D/F#: F#2–A3–D4 | mp, borrowed major light |
| 7 / 13.75 | Bb4 1, C5 1, D5 1 | Bb2/F3 → C3/G3 → D3/A3 | mp → p; b6–b7–1 |
| 8 / 16.00 | E5 3 | D3–A3–E4; no third, let ring | pp |

Composer's critique and revision: the first sketch repeated the same four-note
shape three times; that would merely metrify the rejected fragment cue. Bar 2
now completes a singable falling answer, bar 3 changes its destination, and bar 5
expands the leap and register. An early draft doubled the entire answer; remove
the octaves after bar 5 so the F# can sound intimate. Avoid a bass note on every
beat except the deliberate three-step cadence. The hook can be sung without its
harmony; its risk is that a compact eight-bar period can sound too tidy. Leaving
E over D/A, with no final tonic melody or dominant cadence, keeps the horizon open.

## B — A path in the wind

A minor, **6/8, dotted quarter = 54.054** (eighth = 0.37 s), eight bars from 0.35 s.
A lilting, circular melody, answering in long dotted beats rather than A's dotted
leap rhythm. Climax at **9.23 s**; release at about 19.7 s. Solo piano.

| 1 / 0.35 | E5 1, B4 1, C5 1, E5 3 | Am: A2–E3–C4 | p |
| 2 / 2.57 | D5 2, C5 1, B4 3 | G(add9): G2–D3–A3 | p |
| 3 / 4.79 | E5 1, B4 1, C5 1, G5 2, F5 1 | Fmaj7: F2–C3–A3 | mp |
| 4 / 7.01 | F5 2, E5 1, r 3 | Bb(#11), bII: Bb2–F3–D4; short pedal | p, breath |
| 5 / 9.23 | A5 3, G5 1, E5 1, C5 1 | A2/A3–E4–B4, no third; first note in octaves | mf |
| 6 / 11.45 | C#5 2, E5 1, B4 3 | A(add9): A2–E3–C#4 | mp |
| 7 / 13.67 | F5 2, G5 2, A5 2 | F2/C3 → G2/D3 → A2/E3 | mp → p |
| 8 / 15.89 | E5 6 | A2–E3–B3 | pp |

Composer's critique and revision: the first draft used eighth notes in every bar,
which made a travelling tune too busy for a panorama. Bars 2 and 6 now stop on B,
and bar 4 leaves a whole dotted beat empty. Keep the accompaniment off the second
dotted beat; no rocking ostinato. The circling E–B–C–E figure is hummable and its
G extension gives it direction, but compound meter feels more like setting out
than discovering an enormous landscape. Good alternative if A feels too solemn.

## C — The hills remember

G minor with a parallel-major answer, **4/4, quarter = 100**, eight bars from
0.25 s. Broad hymn with long–short–short rhythm, mostly stepwise and third motion.
Climax at **9.85 s**, release at about 21 s. Solo piano, no pad.

| Bar / start | Melody (quarter-note beats) | Harmony / left hand | Dynamic |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 / 0.25 | D5 2, Bb4 1, C5 1 | Gm: G2–D3–Bb3 | p |
| 2 / 2.65 | D5 3, G4 1 | Ebmaj7: Eb3–Bb3–G4 | p |
| 3 / 5.05 | F5 2, Eb5 1, D5 1 | Cm(add9): C3–G3–Eb4 | mp |
| 4 / 7.45 | C5 1, Ab4 1, r 2 | Ab major, bII: Ab2–Eb3–C4; short pedal | p, breath |
| 5 / 9.85 | G5 2, D5 1, E5 1 | G2/G3–D4–A4, no third; melody octaves | mf |
| 6 / 12.25 | D5 3, B4 1 | G/B: B2–D3–G3 | mp, major light |
| 7 / 14.65 | C5 2, D5 1, G5 1 | Eb3/Bb3 → F3/C4 → G3/D4 | p |
| 8 / 17.05 | A5 4 | G2–D3–A3; no third | pp, distant ninth |

Composer's critique and revision: the first draft stacked close triads under each
long note, which was too much like a generic victory cue. Spread the left hand
and use one harmonic arrival per bar; reserve octaves for bar 5. The final high A
is intentionally softer than the reveal, with no bass crescendo. This is the
simplest melody to hum, but its square meter and hymn character are more settled
than the moving camera. A has the stronger balance of motion and emotional lift.

## Implementation and audition protocol

`BOTW_TITLE_THEME=a|b|c` selects the candidate; unset or unknown selects A.
`title_theme_a/b/c` and their private helpers are isolated from the piano-bank,
field score and other story cues. Dynamics are phrase-shaped velocities, not
post-export normalization. Pedal sustains use the existing bounded note release.
All candidates stay within C2–C7. No synthesized layer: the piano alone provides
the required register and dynamic swell.

Render the production `title` and `cave_exit` fixtures with their existing seed
70070, master 0.8 and ambience; keep full 45-second WAVs and note logs for checks.
The audition excerpts retain the first 22 seconds at unchanged gain. The current
master and original title are references, also without normalization. Meter and
shape are compositional judgments; checks and level measurements cannot decide
whether the user's ear prefers a theme.

Render revision: the first A mix measured -21.1 LUFS-I, above the existing
restrained-title ceiling (-22). Reduce the title-only performance gain by 2.5 dB
for all three candidates, retaining their phrase dynamics and unchanged ambience.
This is production gain, not normalization of the audition exports.

Performance revision: simultaneous melody/bass preparation raised the clean-scene
CPU p99 in the first pair (14.27 to 18.80 ms thread CPU). Let the left hand lead
the beat by 55 ms (45 ms in the walk-up), and spread octave strikes by 25 ms.
The melody stays on its written beats; bass anticipations are performance timing,
not extra pickups. Shorten internal left-hand pedal holds to 1.50 s (A/B) or
1.70 s (C), plus the existing 0.65 s damper release, so each harmony clears before
the next. The final open chord keeps its long release. Re-measure after this
revision; screenshot-encoding frames are excluded by disabling capture hooks,
not by discarding measured outliers.

Second performance revision: the first spacing change still measured 18.99 ms
versus 17.15 ms pooled thread p99. Preserve every pitch, attack and written
duration, but include the existing 650 ms release when calculating legato:
melody hold = written duration minus 500 ms (minimum 120 ms), leaving at least
150 ms of overlap. Internal left-hand holds are now 1.05 s (A/B) or 1.15 s (C),
followed by that same release. This is lighter half-pedal articulation; the final
open bass chord still rings to the original end time. No piano-bank changes.
