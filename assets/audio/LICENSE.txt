University of Iowa Musical Instrument Samples (MIS), created by Lawrence Fritts,
Director of the Electronic Music Studios, University of Iowa.
Steinway & Sons model B; performer Evan Mazunik; technician Michael Cash.
Recorded November 5 and 27, 2001, 2017 Voxman Music Building.
Neumann KM 84 microphones, 16-bit / 44.1 kHz stereo, non-anechoic room.

The library states that its recordings may be downloaded and used for any
projects, without restrictions. This is permission from the library, not a
claim that the recordings have a Creative Commons license.
https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MIS.html
https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MISpiano.html
Accessed 2026-10-09. Source credit/permission HTML is cached outside git in
assets/src/piano/; page hashes are recorded in piano-import.json.

Adaptations: pp, mf and ff notes at C, Eb, Gb and A from octave 2 through 6, plus C7;
attack alignment, gentle 25 Hz high-pass / 11 kHz low-pass filtering, short
attack/end fades, RMS normalization with peak limits, 5-8 second dry tails,
mono downmix and 48 kHz PCM16 conversion. The game applies its stereo reverb.
Original compositions and contextual timing live in game/music.jo.

See piano-import.json for each recording's URL and SHA-256, the output SHA-256,
processing settings and measurements. Rebuild with tools/import_piano.py.
